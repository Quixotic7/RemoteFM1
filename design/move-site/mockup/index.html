FM-1 firmwares × Ableton Move

Your Move, playing FM-1 firmwares.

The M-VAVE FM-1 has a whole library of community firmwares: synths, samplers, sequencers. RemoteFM1 lets them run off the FM-1, and the Ableton Move can now play them, either as a controller for your computer or phone, or with the firmwares running on the Move itself.

03

The words, for newcomers

Eight names come up again and again. Here is each in a line.

FM-1the little synth

M-VAVE's pocket FM synth: 27 keys, a colour screen, seven knobs, a Wi-Fi and Bluetooth radio, and an updater that takes community firmwares. Here it is optional: a panel you can hold.

Ableton Movethe pad instrument

Ableton's standalone groovebox: 32 pads with light, eight knobs, a jog wheel, a small screen, a speaker and Wi-Fi. A small Linux computer inside, which is what makes Route B possible.

Schwungtools for the Move

An independent, open-source add-on for the Move (schwung.dev). It adds a Tools menu and a module manager. Move's own software keeps working beside it. Not made or supported by Ableton.

Schwung Managermove.local:7700

Schwung's web page on the Move. You install tools such as Move Remote and RemoteFM1 there, from any browser on the same Wi-Fi.

Move RemoteRoute A's tool

A Schwung tool that turns the whole Move into a remote surface: its pads, knobs and screen go to an app over Wi-Fi or USB-C. RemoteFM1 is one such app.

RemoteFM1the player and the firmware

Two halves. The player (remotefm1.com, the iOS and Android apps, FM1VST) runs FM-1 firmwares. The RemoteFM1 firmware, flashed on an FM-1, makes the FM-1 a panel for that player: over USB, Bluetooth or Wi-Fi.

RemoteFM1 on the MoveRoute B's tool

The same player as a Schwung tool named RemoteFM1: the firmwares built for the Move's processor, its page at remotefm1.local:7730, three slots, Schwung FX. Its source is the RemoteFM14Move project.

.rfm1 and the catalogthe firmwares

An .rfm1 file is one FM-1 firmware, packed to run in RemoteFM1. The catalog lists every one the authors have published, with its licence and source; the Move downloads them from a signed list, checked file by file.

04

Set it up, step by step

Tick each step as you go; this page remembers where you are. Each step ends with a checkpoint: what you should see before going on.

0/6

About 15 minutes. You need: the Move on Wi-Fi, a computer for Schwung's installer, and Chrome or Edge (or the RemoteFM1 app) to play.

  1. Back up your Sets and samples first. Download Schwung's desktop installer from schwung.dev (Mac, Windows or Linux) and follow it; it sets up the connection to the Move over Wi-Fi and installs.

    CheckpointOn the Move, Shift + Step 13 opens a Tools menu.

  2. On the computer, open move.local:7700 (Schwung Manager) › Modules › Tools › Move Remote › Install.

    CheckpointMove Remote is in the Tools menu.

  3. Shift + Step 13, turn the jog wheel to Move Remote, press it. Move's own software stops while it runs.

    CheckpointThe Move's screen shows its addresses (move.local, and 172.16.254.1 over USB-C).

  4. On remotefm1.com in Chrome or Edge: the menu › Use my Ableton Move. Allow "local network devices" if Chrome asks; other browsers open a small bridge window. In the iOS or Android app: Settings › Ableton Move.

    CheckpointThe pads light up in the FM-1's key colours; the Move's screen shows the firmware.

  5. Choose a firmware in the player. Track buttons 1 to 4 switch the Move between FM-1, MIDI, Control and Sequencer modes.

    CheckpointA pad plays a note from the computer's or phone's speakers.

  6. Hold Shift, touch the volume knob and click the jog wheel: Move's own software comes back.

    CheckpointThe Move is a Move again.

Optional: an FM-1 with RemoteFM1's Bluetooth firmware can be a second panel beside the Move, and play the sound on its own speaker. Flash it.

0/7

About 20 minutes, most of it the first download. You need: the Move on Wi-Fi, a computer for Schwung's installer once, and a phone for the page.

  1. Back up your Sets and samples first. Download Schwung's desktop installer from schwung.dev and follow it.

    CheckpointShift + Step 13 opens a Tools menu on the Move.

  2. Open move.local:7700 › Modules › Tools › RemoteFM1 › Install. Not listed yet? Install Custom Module › From GitHub URL › Quixotic7/remoteFM14Move.

    CheckpointRemoteFM1 is in the Tools menu.

  3. Shift + Step 13 › RemoteFM1. The first launch unpacks the bundled firmwares (about 45 MB) before any sound.

    CheckpointThe screen shows a firmware and the page's address; the pads glow as keys.

  4. On the same Wi-Fi: remotefm1.local:7730 (or move.local:7730; on Android, the address on the Move's screen). It is the FM-1's screen and the whole remotefm1.com look, served by the Move.

    CheckpointThe page's screen moves when you turn a knob on the Move.

  5. Shift + Step 1 opens the projects view: three slots. Mute opens the catalog; press the jog to download. Or the page's Browse all.

    CheckpointA downloaded firmware loads into a slot and plays.

  6. Cable: any FM-1 with RemoteFM1 0.3 or later, into the Move's USB-A port. No cable: flash the Wi-Fi firmware, then join your Wi-Fi.

    CheckpointThe page's pill reads "FM-1 · USB-A" or "FM-1 · Wi-Fi", and the FM-1 shows the firmware.

  7. Hold Shift, touch the volume knob and click the jog wheel. Your projects and states are kept.

    CheckpointMove's own software is back.

07

When something's off

remotefm1.local doesn't open

Use move.local:7730, or the address on the Move's screen. Android's browser often can't resolve .local names. The phone must be on the same Wi-Fi as the Move (not a guest network).

The player can't reach the Move (Route A)

Is Move Remote running (its addresses on the screen)? In Chrome, allow "local network devices" when asked. On a Mac, System Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network › your browser or app on. A USB-C cable to the computer is the fastest and surest link.

The Wi-Fi FM-1 isn't found by the Move

Both must be on the same network, and some routers block devices from seeing each other ("client isolation"). 2.4 GHz only. The FM-1's settings › WI-FI row shows its state and address. A USB-A cable always works.

Which FM-1 firmware do I need for Route A?

None: Route A doesn't need an FM-1. If you want one as well, the USB + Bluetooth build.

Can I go back to stock?

Yes. RemoteFM1's installer puts M-VAVE's V15 back. On the Move, Schwung's installer uninstalls Schwung; Move's own software and your Sets are untouched by these tools.

Is this made by Ableton or M-VAVE?

No. RemoteFM1, Move Remote and Schwung are independent, open-source projects, not approved, endorsed or supported by Ableton or M-VAVE.
