# RemoteFM1 standalone, S4 (STANDALONE-PLAN.md): the arp and the tempo. ARP turns it on (the Arp screen, ARP lit): the
# keys held step at 1/16 of 120 BPM, up, each note 60 % of its step; ARP held: its settings (MODE, RATE, OCTAVES, GATE
# on KN1..KN4); SELECT the tempo (the Tempo screen); a computer's MIDI clock sets the tempo and steps the arp, its
# start restarts the pattern and the loop, its stop stops the loop; an arp recorded into a loop. Both builds.
wait 400
expect sa_bpm 120
tap ARP
expect sa_arp 1
expect sa_screen 13
expect sa_lit_btns >255
# C major held: C4 E4 G4 (keys 7, 11, 14): C4 at once, then E4 125 ms later, G4, C4 again; off after 75 ms
out

wait 20

wait 60
expect_out USB 90 40 5A
wait 125
expect_out USB 90 43 50
wait 125
expect sa_arp_notes 4
wait 30

wait 1000
expect sa_arp_notes 12

key 11 up
key 14 up
wait 200

expect sa_sounding <9
