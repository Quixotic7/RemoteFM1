# RemoteFM1 standalone, S5 (STANDALONE-PLAN.md): HOME cycles the views (Notes, Scope, Keys); SAVE opens the theme menu
# (9 themes: Pixel and the mockup's 8, the screen in each at once, kept across a power cycle); the user's DX7 banks: a
# 32-voice bulk dump over USB or Bluetooth MIDI kept in XDATA0 (USER1..3 after the ROM cartridges, the oldest replaced
# by a fourth, a bad checksum refused, nothing written outside APP_STORE_OK), there after a power cycle. Both builds.
wait 400
expect sa_view 0
# a chord held: the Scope view (HOME), the Keys view (HOME again), back to Notes
key 9 down 100
key 12 down 100
key 16 down 100
key 19 down 100
wait 200
tap HOME
expect sa_view 1
wait 60
shot sa_scope.png
tap HOME
expect sa_view 2
wait 60
shot sa_keys.png
key 9 up
key 12 up
key 16 up
key 19 up
tap HOME
expect sa_view 0
# HOME with an overlay up closes it, the view stays
turn ALGO 1
expect sa_screen 2
tap HOME
expect sa_screen 0
expect sa_view 0
turn ALGO -1
# the theme menu: SAVE; SELECT steps through the 9; the screen is in it at once; SAVE closes it, the theme kept
tap SAVE
expect sa_screen 16
expect sa_menu 1
wait 60
shot sa_theme_pixel.png
turn SELECT 4 100
expect sa_theme 4
wait 60
shot sa_theme_c64.png
turn SELECT 1 100
wait 60
shot sa_theme_c64im.png
turn SELECT 2 100
expect sa_theme 7
wait 60
shot sa_theme_gameboy.png
turn K1 2 100
expect sa_theme 0
turn K1 -1 100
expect sa_theme 8
wait 60
shot sa_theme_synthwave.png
tap SAVE
expect sa_screen 0
expect sa_theme 8
wait 60
shot sa_notes_synthwave.png
tap SAVE
turn SELECT -6 100
tap SAVE
expect sa_theme 2
# a DX7 bank over USB: USER1, its first voice now (the Preset screen), after the ROM cartridges
sa dx7bank 1 usb
expect sa_ub_got 1
expect sa_ubanks 1
expect sa_ub_slot 0
expect sa_screen 1
sa name USR1 V01
wait 300
shot sa_user_bank.png
turn PRESET 3
sa name USR1 V04
# over Bluetooth: USER2; a bad checksum: refused
sa dx7bank 2 ble
expect sa_ubanks 2
expect sa_ub_slot 1
sa name USR2 V01
sa dx7bank 9 usb bad
expect sa_ub_bad 1
expect sa_ubanks 2
# GLO + PRESETS: the banks go on past the ROM's to the user's, and back
press GLO
turn PRESET -40
expect sa_bank 0
expect sa_ub_slot -1
turn PRESET 40
expect sa_ub_slot 1
release GLO
# a third, then a fourth: the oldest (USER1) replaced
sa dx7bank 3 usb
sa dx7bank 4 usb
expect sa_ubanks 3
expect sa_ub_slot 0
sa name USR4 V01
expect sa_ub_refused 0
expect store_outside 0
# kept across a power cycle (the voice and the theme too, saved a little after)
wait 3000
reboot
wait 300
expect sa_ubanks 3
expect sa_ub_slot 0
sa name USR4 V01
expect sa_theme 2
expect sa_view 0
