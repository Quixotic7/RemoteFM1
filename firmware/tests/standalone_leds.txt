# RemoteFM1 standalone: the keys' LEDs for playing with the screen off (the owner, 2026-10-30). GLO held: the MIDI
# channel's white key (1..16 left to right) and the octave's black key lit, in the instrument and in MIDI REMOTE's
# MIDI KEYS; GLO + a white key sets the instrument's channel too (as MIDI KEYS does). In MIDI REMOTE an encoder turned
# in ABSOLUTE, or MASTER moved, shows its value on the 16 white keys for 1.5 s (0 the first, 127 all 16).
# tests/run_tests.sh runs it (both builds).
wait 400
# GLO held: channel 1 (F3), octave 0 (the sixth black key, F#4)

wait 30

expect sa_screen 2
wait 30
expect sa_lit_keys 8256

wait 20

wait 30
