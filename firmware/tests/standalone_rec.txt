# RemoteFM1 standalone: recording as the owner asked (2026-10-30). REC arms a take: its time starts with the first
# note played (nothing else starts it), REC again calls it off. The recording options in two pages (SELECT): TAKE
# (LENGTH, QUANTIZE, LAYER on KN1..KN3) and COUNT (COUNT-IN none / 1 / 2 / 4 bars, METRONOME, its volume on KN1..KN3);
# PRESETS stays the voice there. A count-in of 2 bars with the metronome: 8 clicks through the audio out, then the
# take from the bar, the metronome going on. tests/run_tests.sh runs it (both builds).
wait 400
master 4096
