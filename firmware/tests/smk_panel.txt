# The M-Vave SMK-37 as an FM-1's panel (docs/SMK37.md; fm1-base device/smk_panel.c, BOARD_SMK37), in the SMK-37 build
# of fm1-base's host simulator (BOARD=smk37 ./build.sh --host; tests/run_tests.sh): the keybed's middle 27 keys and
# the 16 pads as the FM-1's keys and white keys, with velocity, the 2 x 6 buttons as the FM-1's by place, K1 MASTER
# and K2..K8 the encoders, < > SELECT, the faders KNOB 1..4, standalone and in a host's session (the panel as plain MIDI, by the FM-1's matrix numbers), and the
# lights: the button LEDs and the pads as the FM-1's key LEDs.

# a fader is its KNOB (fader 1 KNOB 1: bank A's first CC, CC 1); no CC 16..19 of its own. KNOB 1..4 hold absolute CC
