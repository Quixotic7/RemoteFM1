Mouse and touch

Keys and pads: the nearer the front edge you hit, the harder. Drag across the keys to slide.

Knobs: drag up and down, or the mouse wheel. Faders and the mod wheel: drag; the pitch wheel springs back.

Shift-click or right-click holds a button, key or pad down until you click it again.

Computer keyboard

Keys: Z…M and , from C4 (black keys S D G H J), Q…I from C5 (2 3 5 6 7). [ ] move them an octave. Shift plays softer.

What this is

The firmware's simulator, built for the SMK-37: its controls go through the device's own map onto the FM-1's, and the lights come back as the firmware sets them. The key scan, the LCD and LED drivers, USB and Bluetooth are not emulated. Saved settings stay in this browser.
