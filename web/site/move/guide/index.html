GUIDERemoteFM1 on the Move · 0.6.0

Find your way, fast.

Three FM-1 firmwares at once on an Ableton Move, a 16-track sequencer, a session view, a sampler, a kit, projects and states. This page says what is new, how to get around, and shows every shortcut on the Move itself.

01

What's new

The latest releases: 0.6.0 (26 November 2026), 0.5.0 and 0.4.0.

  • The kit

    A 16-pad sampler on Track 4: tap Track 4 round Mixer, Routing, Kit, or hold it + pad 3. The left pads are the kit, each row on its own track, 13 to 16. The right pads play the chosen pad in pitches or chops. Keymap

  • Pages and engines

    The jog turns the pages: Sample Kit, Pad Sample, Pad Sample 2, Trig, Warp, Filter, Pad Mix, Kit Mix. Six engines: One Shot, Momentary, Loop, Pitch Env, LoFi and Granular.

  • Loading sounds

    Press a pad, click the jog: My Samples, with your takes, and the Move Library, Move's own sounds. The sound the jog rests on is on the pad; play the pad to hear it. Browsing never previews.

  • Your samples are never changed

    A pad's sound is copied into the project; edits make new files there. Export Samples (Shift + step 3 on a kit track) copies the kit's sounds into a new folder in My Samples, a file a pad.

  • Copy, Mix, Join on the jog

    Hold Copy and turn the jog for the mode, press the source pad, then each target: done at once. Onto an empty pad, Mix and Join copy.

  • Sequencing with held steps

    A tapped step is a plain hit. Hold a step: a knob locks its value, the volume knob sets its velocity, the jog its length, Left / Right its timing. A lock on a step without a hit is a ghost trigger. Delete + touch a knob clears that knob's locks; with a step held, just that step's.

  • Levels in dB

    Every level, a firmware's, the kit's or a pad's, shows the same popup in dB, 0.5 dB a detent.

  • Shut Down?

    Press the power button, click the jog: RemoteFM1 saves, then the Move turns off. Holding it still works.

  • Update and Listen here

    The page's settings sheet installs a signed .rfm1u build, with Undo last update. The headphones button plays the Move on the phone or iPad with the page open.

  • Exit

    The last row of Global settings: click, then click again to leave RemoteFM1.

  • A sampler

    Sample opens it. It records the master, one firmware on its own, the audio input, or stems: the master and each firmware as separate files. Keymap

  • Four ways to record

    Capture keeps what you just played. Bars starts and ends on the bar line. Threshold starts with the sound. Manual starts and ends at your press.

  • Takes in the Move's own library

    Every take is a WAV file in Samples/RemoteFM1, a folder a day, so Move's sample browser lists it. Firmwares that read audio, like Elasticat, see the same takes as audio/rec.

  • Play, trim and edit a take

    The Takes tab lists everything you recorded. A click on a take: Play, Trim, Normalize, Reverse, Fade In, Fade Out, Delete, with one step of Undo. Keymap

  • The sampler on your phone

    The Move's page has a Sampler window next to Mix & FX: the settings and the record key, your takes with playback, the same edits, rename, download and delete.

  • Minus in the FM-1 layout

    It no longer switches firmware: the Track buttons do that.

  • Engine parameters on the knobs

    A new knob page, ENGINE: a firmware's own sound on the Move's eight knobs, by name and value, nothing to set up. ChoralRoot 0.46 is first: both layers, seven engines, 632 parameters on 117 pages. Keymap

  • Names and values for 59 more firmwares

    Touch or turn a knob in the FM-1 layout and the Move says what it is and its value: Melodee, Felucca, SLOOP, GR!MM and many more.

  • States that belong to the project

    Pad 1 of a firmware's States is the open project's own sound, saved as you play. Pads 2 to 32 are a library every project shares. Keymap

  • States and Projects on your phone

    The Move's page, remotefm1.local:7730, has two new panels: load, save, download, upload and delete states and projects.

  • More rates, and triplets

    A track's rate goes from 1/32 to 4 Bars, with a Feel: Straight, Dotted or Triplet. In Triplet every fourth step key is off, so each group of three is one triplet. Shift + a Track opens its settings.

  • The arp plays your clips

    Each track has its own arp, fed by the clip's notes as well as the pads. Octaves go down as well as up, and a jog click makes a random Mod or Transpose pattern. Keymap

  • The firmware's knobs, one click away

    On the FM-1 knob page one jog click hands the steps and buttons to the firmware too (Knobs + Keys). Back leaves. Back + Play is always the Move's own Play. Keymap

  • Random, on step 13

    In the firmware list: a random firmware from the list you are in. On the Projects page: a new project with three random firmwares and a random key.

The full lists: RemoteFM14Move's releases. Projects saved by 0.6.0 do not open in 0.5.0 or older; older projects load as they were.

02

Getting around

Eight things, in the order you meet them. The four Track buttons beside the pads are the way to everything.

  1. Three firmwares, three Track buttons

    Track 1, 2 and 3 are the three slots: orange, blue, yellow. Tap one to play its firmware. Double tap it to choose a firmware: a pad picks one, the jog's click loads it. Hold it for its menu.

  2. Every slot has a layout

    Hold the Track and press a pad of the top row. FM-1: the pads are the FM-1's keys, the knobs and buttons the firmware's own. Own: the firmware's own Move layout, when it brings one (ChoralRoot does). Scale Chroma Drum Octave: the note layouts.

  3. A note layout is the sequencer

    The pads play the selected track, the 16 steps are its clip, Play starts it. There are 16 tracks, shared by the three firmwares: hold a Track and press a step to choose the track that firmware plays.

  4. The jog turns the knob pages

    TRIG, FM-1, ENGINE, then the CC banks. Touch any knob and the screen names it and shows its value. Each track remembers its page.

  5. Menu is session view

    All the clips on the pads: a column a track, the last column the scenes. Menu again takes you back.

  6. Track 4 is everything else

    Tap: the Mixer, again for Routing (your Schwung effects), again for the Kit. Double tap: the 32 projects. Hold + a step: the Shift shortcuts, from anywhere.

  7. Back only closes

    One click closes the last menu or latch. It never changes where you are. Lost? Back + Delete closes everything, then tap a Track.

  8. Leaving

    Shift + touch the volume knob + click the jog, or Exit, the last row of Global settings. Move's own software starts again. The power button asks Shut Down?: the jog's click saves and turns the Move off.

Only Play starts it

Steps, pads, menus and loading never start or stop the transport. A scene pad is the one exception.

Click, hold, double

A quick click on Loop or Mute latches it; a hold lasts while you hold. A Track: tap, hold, double tap.

Lit knobs do something

A dark knob is unused where you are. Touch a lit one to read its name before turning it.

The screen answers

Every press names what it did. With Speech on (Global settings), the Move says it too.
