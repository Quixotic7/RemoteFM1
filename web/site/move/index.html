03

The words, for newcomers

Eight names come up again and again. Here is each in a line.

FM-1the little synth

M-VAVE's pocket FM synth: 27 keys, a colour screen, seven knobs, a Wi-Fi and Bluetooth radio, and an updater that takes community firmwares. Here it is optional: a panel you can hold.

Ableton Movethe pad instrument

Ableton's standalone instrument: 32 pads that light, eight knobs, a jog wheel, a small screen, a speaker and Wi-Fi. A small Linux computer inside, which is what makes Route B possible.

Schwungtools for the Move

An independent, open-source add-on for the Move (schwung.dev). It adds a Tools menu and a module manager; Move's own software keeps working beside it. Not made, approved or supported by Ableton.

Schwung Managermove.local:7700

Schwung's web page on the Move. You install tools such as Move Remote and RemoteFM1 there, from a browser on the same Wi-Fi.

RemoteFM1the player and the firmware

Two halves. The player (remotefm1.com, the iOS and Android apps, FM1VST in a DAW) runs FM-1 firmwares. The RemoteFM1 firmware, flashed on an FM-1, makes the FM-1 a panel for a player: over USB, Bluetooth or Wi-Fi.

RemoteFM1 on the MoveRoute B's tool

The player as a Schwung tool named RemoteFM1: the firmwares built for the Move's processor, its page at remotefm1.local:7730, three slots, your Schwung effects. Its source is the RemoteFM14Move project.

Move RemoteRoute A's tool

A Schwung tool that turns the whole Move into a remote surface: its pads, knobs and screen go to an app over Wi-Fi or USB-C. RemoteFM1 is one such app (source).

.rfm1 and the catalogthe firmwares

An .rfm1 file is one FM-1 firmware, packed to run in RemoteFM1. The catalog lists every one the authors have published, with its licence and source; the Move downloads them from a signed list, checked file by file.

07

When something's off

remotefm1.local doesn't open

Use move.local:7730, or the address on the Move's screen. Android's browser often can't resolve .local names. The phone must be on the same Wi-Fi as the Move (not a guest network).

The player can't reach the Move (Route A)

Is Move Remote running (its addresses on the screen)? In Chrome, allow "local network devices" when asked. On a Mac, System Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network › your browser or app on. A USB-C cable to the computer is the fastest and surest link.

The Wi-Fi FM-1 isn't found by the Move

Both must be on the same network, and some routers keep devices from seeing each other ("client isolation"). 2.4 GHz only. The FM-1's settings › WI-FI row shows its state and address. A USB-A cable always works.

Which FM-1 firmware do I need for Route A?

None: Route A doesn't need an FM-1. If you want one as well, the USB + Bluetooth build.

Can I go back?

Yes. remotefm1.com/installer puts the USB + Bluetooth build back over the Wi-Fi one, or M-VAVE's V15. On the Move, Schwung's installer uninstalls Schwung; your Sets stay.

Is this made by Ableton or M-VAVE?

No. RemoteFM1, Move Remote and Schwung are independent, open-source projects, not approved, endorsed or supported by Ableton or M-VAVE.
